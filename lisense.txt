
### **5. How to Download:**

1. **Create a new folder** called `school-website`

2. **Copy each file's content** into separate files:
   - `index.html`
   - `style.css`
   - `script.js`
   - `README.md` (optional)

3. **Add images** (optional):
   - Create an `images` folder
   - Add your logo as `logo.png`
   - Add other images as referenced in the HTML
